How SwapEd Works

Designed around campus schedules—no shipping delays, no middlemen, and no platform commissions.

STEP 01
Browse Items

Explore textbooks, lab coats, calculators, and drafting kits listed by verified seniors and batchmates.

STEP 02
Find What You Need

Filter by exact Thapar course code (e.g., UCS301, UTA015), item condition, and price range.

STEP 03
Reserve for Pickup

Place a one-click pickup reservation request. Once the seller accepts, the item is locked for you.

STEP 04
Meet & Complete Trade

Meet at the Central Library, COS Complex, or hostel gate, inspect the gear, and record the completed trade.
