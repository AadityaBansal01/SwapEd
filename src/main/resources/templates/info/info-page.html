SwapEd Campus Platform

Information

SwapEd — Campus Student Book & Gear Exchange is a peer-to-peer academic marketplace engineered exclusively for students of Thapar Institute of Engineering & Technology (TIET), Patiala.

Our mission is to solve a recurring student challenge: the high cost of single-semester textbooks, lab coats, mini drafters, and scientific calculators. By connecting seniors and juniors within a verified @thapar.edu network, SwapEd enables zero-commission campus pickups and sustainable resource reuse.

1. How do I verify my @thapar.edu account?

Register using your official Thapar email address (e.g., rjain1_be24@thapar.edu). Click the single-use verification link sent to your email to activate your account.

2. How do reservations and trades work?

Click Reserve for Pickup on any available item. The seller receives your request in their dashboard and can accept or decline it. Once accepted, the item is marked Reserved while you meet at the listed campus pickup spot. After handover, the seller clicks Confirm Handover to record the completed trade.

3. Can I cancel a reservation?

Yes. Both buyers and sellers can cancel a pending or accepted reservation prior to handover, which automatically restores the listing to Available status.

SwapEd collects only essential student identity details (Full Name, official @thapar.edu email address, Roll Number, Department, and Course) required to verify Thapar campus membership and facilitate safe peer-to-peer exchanges.

All passwords are encrypted using salted BCrypt hashes. Verification and password reset tokens are stored exclusively as SHA-256 cryptographic hashes and are never exposed in plain text in the database.

  • Only currently enrolled Thapar University students with a valid @thapar.edu email may register, list, or reserve items.
  • All item condition ratings (New, Like New, Good, Fair) must accurately describe the physical state of the textbook or equipment.
  • Exchanges must take place in public campus areas (Central Library, COS Complex, Academic Blocks, or Hostel Gates) during daylight/campus hours.
  • Students may only modify or delete their own listings and may not reserve their own items.
